Support

Last updated: April 2, 2026

Velum is an iPhone app for numerology, astrology, tarot, and runes. It requires Apple Intelligence on a compatible iPhone.

Common setup checks

  • Make sure you are using a compatible iPhone.
  • Update to the latest supported iOS release for your device.
  • Open Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri and complete setup.
  • If Apple Intelligence is still preparing, wait for the process to finish and try again.

Location help

Velum only asks for location access when you use present-moment astrology features. If you do not want to share your current location, you can enter a location manually in the app.

Privacy

Velum stores your saved profile and reading history on your device. Read the Privacy Policy for current details.
