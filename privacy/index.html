Velum

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 16, 2026

Velum is an iPhone app for personal reflection through numerology, astrology, tarot, and runes. This policy explains what information the app handles and how that information is used.

What Velum stores

Velum stores the following information on your device so the app can work:

  • Birth profile details you enter, such as date, time, and place of birth
  • Reading history and journal-style content generated inside the app
  • App preferences such as your last-selected tab and text-to-speech settings
  • Astrology context such as saved chart snapshots and manually chosen locations

Apple Intelligence

Velum requires Apple Intelligence on a compatible iPhone. Interpretations are generated with Apple-provided on-device AI features. Velum is designed to stay locked until Apple Intelligence is available, enabled, and ready on the device.

Location data

Velum asks for location access only when you choose features that depend on your current sky, such as present-moment astrology readings. If you prefer, you can enter a location manually instead.

Location lookups and reverse geocoding may use Apple system services that are part of iOS. Velum does not require location permission to use the rest of the app.

Data sharing

Based on the current app implementation, Velum does not include a user account system, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or a custom backend service for transmitting your profile or generated readings off-device.

Data retention and deletion

Because your saved content is stored locally on your device, you can remove it by deleting your saved entries inside the app where available, clearing app data through the app's controls when provided, or uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

Velum is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published before the related app update is submitted or released.
